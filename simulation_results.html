Interpretation guide

High overlap + high τ Community largely agrees with AI — same images, similar order
Low overlap + low τ Community strongly disagrees — different images, different order
High overlap + low τ Community picks the same images but orders them differently — disagrees on priority
Low overlap + high τ Community picks different images but orders their selection similarly to how AI ordered them
